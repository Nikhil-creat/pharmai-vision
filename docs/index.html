PharmAi Vision

Agentic assistant with tool calling (FDA labels, interactions, FAERS adverse events, recalls, cabinet, memory), self-check guard against unverified doses, vision scanning of packs and pills, medicine cabinet with reminders, voice in English/Telugu/Hindi, emergency escalation, and printable briefings.

The full production blueprint (CNN+ViT, LangGraph, Neo4j GraphRAG, Docker/K8s) is in the repository; this page is the free, GitHub-Pages edition.

Privacy: your Groq key, profile, cabinet and chat history are stored only in this browser. Prompts go directly to Groq; only public FDA lookups go elsewhere.

⚕ Educational information only — not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. AI identification can be wrong. Never start, stop or change a medicine based on this tool; confirm with a pharmacist or doctor. Emergency: call your local number (US 911 · EU 112 · India 112) or US Poison Control 1-800-222-1222.
