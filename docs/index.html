Identify a tablet from a photo

Take a clear photo on a plain background (imprint facing the camera). The vision model describes the pill; always verify the imprint with a pharmacist.

Data: U.S. FDA drug labels (openFDA). The AI may only use label text; every dose figure is checked against it.

Interaction checker

Enter 2+ medicines separated by commas. Each FDA label's interaction section is scanned for the others.

PharmAi Vision

Futuristic medicine-information assistant: vision AI pill description, FDA-grounded RAG briefings, interaction scanning, voice (Groq Whisper + speech synthesis), emergency escalation, and a hallucination guard that verifies every dose figure against the label.

The full production blueprint (CNN+ViT, LangGraph agents, Neo4j GraphRAG, Docker/K8s) ships in the repository — this page is the free, GitHub-Pages-hostable edition.

Privacy: your Groq key and health profile are stored only in this browser (localStorage). Prompts go directly to Groq; nothing is sent to any other server except public FDA lookups.

⚕ Educational information only — not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Pill identification by AI can be wrong. Never take or stop a medicine based on this tool; confirm with a pharmacist or doctor. In an emergency call your local emergency number (US 911 · EU 112 · India 112) or US Poison Control 1-800-222-1222.
